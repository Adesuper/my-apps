How each piece moves

PieceHow it moves
♟️ PawnForward 1 square (2 on its first move). Captures diagonally.
♜ RookStraight lines — up, down, left, right — as far as it likes.
♝ BishopDiagonals only, as far as it likes. Stays on one colour.
♞ KnightAn “L” shape: 2 + 1. The only piece that jumps over others!
♛ QueenAny direction, any distance. The most powerful piece.
♚ KingOne square in any direction. Protect it — if it’s trapped, the game ends.
