What division is really asking

Every division question is really asking one of two things:

"How many does each group get?"  or  "How many equal groups can I make?"

Example: 12 ÷ 3 means "share 12 things equally among 3" → each gets 4. Same idea works whether the numbers are small or big.

💡 Keep this in your head: the ÷ sign means "split into equal groups." That's the whole trick — the rest is just finding the number that fits.

LEFTOVERS

🍩 What if it doesn't share evenly?

Sometimes there are leftovers that can't be shared fairly. In math we call the leftover the remainder and write r.

13 ÷ 4 = 3 r 1

You share 13 treats among 4 friends → everyone gets 3, and 1 treat is left over. Try 13 treats and 4 friends in the machine above to see it! 👆

SECRET SHORTCUT

🔁 Division is just backwards multiplication

Here's a superpower: if you know your times tables, you already know division! They're opposites, like a lock and a key. 🔑

4 × 3 = 12
3 × 4 = 12
12 ÷ 3 = 4
12 ÷ 4 = 3

So when you see 12 ÷ 3 = ?, just ask yourself:

"3 times WHAT makes 12?" 🤔 → 3 × 4 = 12. Answer: 4!

💡 Stuck on any division? Flip it into a "times what?" question. Your times tables are the key that unlocks it.
BIG NUMBERS TRICK

🧱 Sharing BIG numbers: the Chunk Method

Your homework has big numbers like 336 ÷ 12. Sharing 336 cookies one-by-one would take forever! So we share in big chunks instead. 🚛

Example: 336 ÷ 12 (share 336 cookies among 12 friends)

Give everyone a chunk at a time, and keep track of how much each friend has gotten:

Start with 336 cookies.
Give each friend 20 → that's 12 × 20 = 240 used.each: 20
336 − 240 = 96 cookies left.
Give each friend 8 more → 12 × 8 = 96 used.each: +8
96 − 96 = 0 cookies left. All shared! 🎉
Add up what each friend got: 20 + 8= 28

336 ÷ 12 = 28 ✅

💡 Trick to find chunks fast: write a mini times-table for the divisor first.
12: ×1=12, ×2=24, ×5=60, ×10=120, ×20=240. Now you can grab the biggest chunk that fits!

Sheet 2 — Word Problems (find the hidden ÷)

1) How many seventies make 140?
This asks 140 ÷ 70. "70 times what = 140?" → 2
2) 80 people per bus. How many buses for 560 people?
560 ÷ 80 = 7 buses 🚌
3) A book costs ₦75. Ade spent ₦750. How many books?
750 ÷ 75 = 10 books 📚
4) A boat carries 70 per round. How many rounds for 630 people?
630 ÷ 70 = 9 rounds ⛵
5) 3 girls + 4 boys share 483 sweets equally. How many each?
First add the children: 3 + 4 = 7. Then 483 ÷ 7 = 69 sweets each 🍬
6) A tailor bought 9 metres of cloth for ₦2 520. Cost per metre?
2520 ÷ 9 = ₦280 per metre 🧵
7) How many hundreds are there in 2 000?
2000 ÷ 100 = 20
🕵️ Word-problem detective tip: words like "share equally", "each", "how many groups", "per" almost always mean DIVIDE.
