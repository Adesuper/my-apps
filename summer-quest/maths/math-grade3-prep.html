CARD 2

✖️ Times tables — smart order

Learn them in this order (easy → tricky): 2, 5, 10 → 4 (double the 2s) → 3 → 9 → squares (2×2, 3×3…) → 6, 7, 8.

💡 Once you know 0, 1, 2, 5 and 10, only a few facts are “hard”: 3×6, 3×7, 3×8, 4×6, 4×7, 4×8, 6×7, 6×8, 7×8. And 4×6 = 6×4 — so you only learn each one once!
